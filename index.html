Day One

Raise islands out of an empty sea. Carry earth, drop a layer on every space you cross, and claim the land where your god comes to rest.

Game log
    Rulebook

    Each player is an island god raising a new world out of an empty sea. Your god carries stacks of earth across the waves, drops a layer on every space it crosses, and plants shrines where it comes to rest.

    Setup: the board is a tight 6 × 7 sea with 10 mountains printed on the corners where three spaces meet (3 points each) and printed pearl spots (shuffle the 1–3 pearls onto them). With 2 or 3 players, play only inside the dashed zone and leave the shallows outside it empty. Pick any player colours. Each player takes a god, a scoring cube and their shrines (7 / 6 / 5 / 4 with 2 / 3 / 4 / 5 players). The board has 5 numbered start spaces: the first player starts on 1, the second on 2, and so on, with one shrine on the space and their god standing on it. Put a grey stone shrine on every numbered space nobody is using inside the play area (2 players: spaces 3 and 5; 3 players: space 5; 4 players: space 5; 5 players: none). Put earth discs of each of the 5 land types in the supply: 10 each with 4 or 5 players (the full box, 50 discs), 8 with 3, 7 with 2. Each player also takes a player board and puts a marker at 0 on each of its five land-type tracks.

    1. Gather earth: top up the stack under your god to 2 discs of one land type from the supply (if it already holds 2 or more, add 1). Your god stands on top. If your god can't travel from there, start from one of your other shrines instead.
    2. Travel: lift the whole stack and move it one space at a time, in any direction, dropping the bottom disc on each space you enter. You can't enter the same space twice in a turn. Mountains sit on corners, so they never block the way. Your god lands last, on a space with no shrine.
    3. Offerings: a disc that would drop onto a rival's shrine is an offering. Drop it on top of their shrine (their island grows). They move that land type's marker up 1 on their player board, and you move your cube 1 space along the score track. Pearls offered go straight to them. Give rivals what they don't need. A disc dropped on a stone shrine is an offering too: you score 1, nobody's track moves, and a pearl dropped there goes back to the box.
    4. Eruption (once per turn): if your god lands on a stack of 2 or more discs with no shrine, and at least one disc in it is the land type you took this turn, the island erupts. Collect its top disc, add 1 disc of your land type, and travel again.
    5. Raise a shrine: collect the top disc where your god lands (an empty space gives nothing), then slip a shrine under your god. If all your shrines are already on the sea, move one of them here instead; the stack it stood on stays behind.
    6. Seal mountains: each mountain touches exactly 3 spaces. The moment all 3 hold shrines, it is sealed: the player with the most shrines there takes the mountain tile off the board (3 points). If players tie, the tile goes back to the box. After that, those shrines are free to move on to the next mountain.

    Collecting: whenever you collect an earth disc (the top disc where your god lands, or the top disc in an eruption), move that land type's marker up 1 on your player board (tracks stop at 25) and put the disc back in the supply. Pearls you keep.

    Game end (the sea is full): earth only leaves the supply for the sea, and only comes back when someone collects it, so the supply slowly empties as the islands grow. The game ends the moment a player cannot gather earth, because no land type in the supply has enough discs.

    • Land types: for each land type, the player furthest along that track scores 10 and the clear second scores 5. If players tie for the most, each of them scores 5 and there is no second place.
    • Pearls score their printed value.
    • Offerings: 1 point for every offering you made (your cube on the track).
    • Mountains: 3 points for each mountain you sealed and took. A mountain still open at the end goes to the player with the most shrines around it (a tie scores nothing).
    • Tiebreak: most discs collected.
    Hover a space to read its stack, bottom to top.

    Designer settings

    Defaults from bot testing: tight 6 × 7 sea with 10 corner mountains worth 3 (6 inside the 2–3 god zone) and 14 pearl spots (11 inside the zone), offerings score 1 for the giver and raise the receiver's island, collected discs go back to the supply and the game ends when the sea is full, 7 / 8 / 10 / 10 discs per land type with 2 / 3 / 4 / 5 gods, 7 / 6 / 5 / 4 shrines. The open-sea option keeps the previous rules: 8 × 9 with 7 peaks (worth 5, scored at the end for the most shrines around them), 14 pearls, 6 / 5 / 4 shrines per god with 3 / 4 / 5 gods, 5 land types with 27 discs each (23 with 3 gods), fill to 2, eruption on stacks of 2+ that contain the land type you took. Lands score 10 for the most and 5 for a clear second (tied for most: 5 each).
